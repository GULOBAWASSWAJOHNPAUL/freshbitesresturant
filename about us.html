Welcome to Fresh Bites

Good food brings people together

Fresh Bites Restaurant is a welcoming neighbourhood restaurant located on Kira Road in Kampala, Uganda. We serve wholesome, flavourful meals prepared with quality ingredients and care.

Whether you are enjoying a quick lunch, meeting friends, celebrating a special occasion, or relaxing over dinner, Fresh Bites is here to make every bite count.

Our goal is simple: to provide delicious food, friendly service, and a comfortable atmosphere where every customer feels welcome.
